All textures must be standard-conforming RGBA 16x16 PNG images.

inventory.png and text.png are used for what you think.
The rest are named according to resources/data/blocks.txt (block name + optional axis + .png).

If your filesystem does not allow differing filenames by capitalization only
(e.g. grassY and grassy give a filename conflict), you can just repeat the same
axis to mitigate this (e.g. grassY and grassyy).
